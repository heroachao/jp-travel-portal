01 Quick decision

Quick answer: Choose a pace that protects the best experiences

A sustainable Japan itinerary usually needs fewer major stops than a map-based estimate suggests, especially in large cities and heritage districts. Fast pacing increases taxi use, convenience purchases, missed reservations, and the risk that one delay damages several later plans.

Next action
Limit each day to one main area or decision, add a nearby secondary stop, and leave meals and transit as real parts of the schedule.
