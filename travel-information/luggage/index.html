Quick answer: Confirm both hotels before forwarding luggage

Treat station lockers as limited capacity, not a reservation

01 Quick decision

Quick answer: Treat station lockers as limited capacity, not a reservation

Station lockers are useful but popular sizes can fill, and a large station may place the available locker far from the next platform or exit. Locker cost is reasonable only when the detour and retrieval time do not create another transport or ticket expense.

Separate an overnight bag before sending the suitcase

01 Quick decision

Quick answer: Separate an overnight bag before sending the suitcase

Forwarding works best when the traveler carries everything needed until the confirmed delivery date rather than reopening or intercepting the main bag. A small day bag can prevent emergency shopping and protects the trip from a normal one-day delivery gap.

Next action
