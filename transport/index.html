Common mistakes

  • Buying a national rail pass without calculating exact route legs.
  • Choosing airport transport only by price when arriving late with luggage.
  • Planning tight train transfers on the first day after a long flight.
  • Ignoring oversized luggage rules and station stairs.
  • Assuming every useful train, subway, or bus is covered by one pass.

FAQ

Practical questions before you book.

Is the JR Pass always worth it?

No. It depends on exact intercity rail legs, travel window, pass price, and whether non-JR transport is needed. Use the checker before buying.

What transport decision should I make first?

Start with arrival airport and overnight bases. Those decide whether rail passes, airport trains, buses, taxis, or luggage forwarding matter.

What should every transport article link to?

Every transport article should link back to this Transport Hub and to the tool that answers the practical decision.

Is Narita or Haneda easier for Tokyo?

Haneda is usually closer to central Tokyo, but Narita can still be practical when the fare is better or the hotel is near a direct train or bus stop.

Do I need an IC card in Japan?

Most travelers benefit from one because it simplifies local trains, buses, lockers, vending machines, and small purchases. Keep some cash as a backup.

When should I use luggage forwarding?

Use forwarding when you have large suitcases, multiple hotel changes, rural stops, crowded train transfers, or an overnight bag strategy.
