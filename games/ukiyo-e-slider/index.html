Ukiyo-e Slider Puzzle strategy

Ukiyo-e Slider Puzzle is designed as a short, repeatable browser game for Japan travel readers. The theme keeps the game connected to trip planning while the rules stay familiar enough for visitors to understand immediately.

Use it as a quick break between destination research, route planning, and transport checks. The best-score loop is stored in your browser so returning visitors can try to improve their result later.

Why travelers may come back

  • Move-count challenge creates a simple reason to replay.
