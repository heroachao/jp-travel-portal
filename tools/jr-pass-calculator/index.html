JR Pass Value Checker

Compare common intercity rail legs against a pass price you can update before purchase.

Output Value estimate Runs locally in your browser
Common one-way rail legs

What problem this tool solves

A rail pass is valuable only when its eligible journeys and valid travel window fit the actual route. This checker totals selected intercity legs against a pass price you enter, so you can see whether the comparison is close enough to require a detailed official fare check.

Example use case

A traveler can add Tokyo-Kyoto, Kyoto-Hiroshima, and Hiroshima-Tokyo, then compare that rough total with the current pass price before deciding whether convenience or individual tickets make more sense.

Who this is for

  • Travelers with several long-distance rail journeys inside one fixed period.
  • Visitors comparing a nationwide pass with individual tickets or a regional pass.
  • Route planners who want to test different city orders before purchase.

Common mistakes

  • Adding journeys that are not covered by the pass being considered.
  • Using an old pass price or an approximate fare from a blog.
  • Ignoring seat-reservation conditions, train exclusions, or private railway sections.
  • Buying a pass before the hotel bases and intercity dates are stable.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official JR fare calculator?

No. It is a preliminary comparison tool. Verify the current pass price, eligible trains, fare components, validity period, and reservation rules on the relevant official JR pages.

Should local Tokyo and Osaka trains be included?

Include only journeys relevant to the specific pass comparison. Small local fares rarely rescue an otherwise poor-value long-distance plan, and some local services are not JR.

What if the result is close?

When totals are close, compare convenience, flexibility, reservation access, and non-covered sections rather than treating a small estimated saving as decisive.

Can this compare regional passes?

You can enter a regional pass price and the journeys you expect it to cover, but you must check that every selected leg falls inside the official coverage area and validity period.

Do reserved-seat charges matter?

They can. Fare structures and pass benefits vary, so use the same seating assumptions on both sides of the comparison and confirm them with the operator.

When should I make the final decision?

After fixing the intercity dates, main hotel bases, and any flights. Recheck prices and rules shortly before purchase because transport products can change.
