IC Card Checklist

Plan IC card setup, cash backup, airport arrival, and first-day transit habits.

Output Payment checklist Runs locally in your browser

What problem this tool solves

An IC card simplifies many local journeys and small payments, but availability, mobile-wallet compatibility, refund rules, and coverage are not identical for every traveler. This checklist organizes the questions to settle before arrival and keeps cash and ticket alternatives visible.

Example use case

An iPhone user landing at Haneda can check mobile-wallet readiness, a physical-card backup, first-day cash, and the separate tickets still needed for reserved or long-distance travel.

Who this is for

  • First-time visitors preparing for local trains, subways, buses, and small purchases.
  • Travelers deciding between a mobile IC card and a physical card.
  • Visitors moving between regions who need to separate local payment from intercity reservations.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming an IC card is an unlimited transport pass.
  • Relying on one phone without a battery, payment, or physical backup.
  • Expecting every rural route, highway bus, or reserved train to accept the same tap payment.
  • Loading a large balance without checking refund and remaining-balance options.

Frequently asked questions

Is an IC card the same as a rail pass?

No. An IC card usually stores value for eligible pay-as-you-go transport and purchases; it does not automatically provide unlimited travel or cover every reserved service.

Can one card be used across several cities?

Major interoperable cards work widely, but exceptions remain. Check the operator for rural lines, special buses, or routes outside the main compatibility area.

Should every traveler have a separate card?

Usually each person needs a separate card for gated transport because one card cannot normally process several passengers through the same gate. Check child-card arrangements separately.

Is a mobile IC card always available to overseas visitors?

Compatibility depends on the phone, wallet region, operating system, and payment method. Test setup before relying on it and keep another payment option.

How much value should I load?

Start with a practical amount for the first day or two, then top up as needed. Avoid treating a large balance as a travel budget.

What still needs a separate ticket?

Reserved seats, some limited express services, Shinkansen arrangements, passes, and non-compatible transport may require a separate ticket or linked reservation.
