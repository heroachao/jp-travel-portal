Luggage Forwarding Planner

Decide when to carry bags, use lockers, or forward luggage between hotels.

Output Luggage plan Runs locally in your browser

What problem this tool solves

Large bags can turn otherwise simple station transfers into slow, stressful journeys. This planner compares hotel changes, bag count, train transfers, lockers, overnight-bag needs, and forwarding so each travel day has a clear luggage plan rather than a last-minute station problem.

Example use case

A traveler moving from Tokyo to Kyoto through Hakone can send the large suitcase ahead, carry one overnight bag, and verify that both hotels accept the service and delivery timing.

Who this is for

  • Travelers changing hotels during an intercity route.
  • Families, winter visitors, and shoppers carrying large or multiple bags.
  • Anyone using stations with long walks, stairs, or tight transfers.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming forwarding is always same-day.
  • Sending everything needed for the next night, medication, or travel documents.
  • Failing to confirm that the sending and receiving properties handle luggage services.
  • Relying on station lockers without checking size, availability, and collection deadlines.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should luggage be sent?

Delivery time depends on origin, destination, service, cutoff, weather, and remote-area conditions. Confirm the actual delivery date with the provider before handing over the bag.

Will every hotel accept forwarded luggage?

No. Confirm both the sending and receiving property, the reservation name, arrival date, payment method, and any storage limits.

What should stay in an overnight bag?

Keep medication, documents, valuables, chargers, weather layers, and everything needed until the forwarded bag is confirmed to arrive.

Are station lockers a guaranteed backup?

No. Large lockers can be full, access hours vary, and some stations are difficult to navigate with bags. Identify another staffed storage option when the timing matters.

Do trains have unlimited luggage space?

No. Space and reservation rules vary by service and bag size. Check the operator before traveling with oversized baggage.

When is carrying the bag simplest?

Carrying can be simplest for one small bag, a direct route, and a hotel close to the station. Reconsider when transfers, stairs, crowds, or oversized-baggage rules add friction.
