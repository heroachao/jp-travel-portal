Airport Transfer Finder

Choose an arrival airport, hotel area, and priority to get a simple transfer shortlist.

Output Transfer shortlist Runs locally in your browser

What problem this tool solves

The fastest airport train is not always the easiest route to a hotel. This tool compares the arrival airport, hotel area, luggage level, time of day, and your priority so the shortlist reflects transfers, walking, and late-arrival risk as well as headline journey time.

Example use case

Two travelers landing in the evening with large suitcases can prioritize fewer transfers to a Shinjuku hotel, then compare that shortlist with the current terminal timetable and final station exit.

Who this is for

  • First-time arrivals who have not used Japanese rail stations before.
  • Travelers landing late, carrying large luggage, or traveling with children.
  • Visitors comparing an airport train, limousine bus, local rail, or taxi for the complete hotel journey.

Common mistakes

  • Comparing only airport-to-station time instead of terminal-to-hotel time.
  • Assuming every service stops at every terminal or hotel.
  • Ignoring the last practical departure after immigration and baggage collection.
  • Choosing a cheap route that requires difficult stairs, long walks, or several transfers with luggage.

Frequently asked questions

Does the finder use live timetables?

No. It organizes route types and priorities. Check the airport and operator websites for the exact terminal, date, last departure, disruptions, and ticket method.

Is a taxi always easiest with luggage?

A taxi reduces transfers but can cost much more and travel time can vary with traffic. Compare the complete group cost and hotel access before deciding.

Why does the hotel area matter?

Different airport services terminate in different parts of a city. A route that is excellent for Ueno may be inconvenient for Shinjuku or a hotel far from the named station.

How much arrival buffer should I allow?

Allow for immigration, baggage, customs, orientation, and ticket collection. Use the current final practical departure as a safety boundary rather than planning around the scheduled landing time.

Should I buy tickets before flying?

It depends on the operator, arrival uncertainty, and reservation rules. Check whether advance purchase is useful, changeable, or risky if the flight is delayed.

What should I verify for a late arrival?

Confirm terminal hours, final train or bus, the station-to-hotel walk, hotel check-in policy, and a backup route you can afford.
