Shikoku Pilgrimage and Nature Guide

A first Shikoku guide for pilgrimage culture, literary routes, islands, nature, and slower regional travel.

Published June 18, 2026 Updated June 18, 2026 1 min read Travel Japan: Shikoku

A slower island for repeat visitors and careful first-timers

Shikoku is often less crowded than Japan's best-known tourist corridors. Official travel sources describe pilgrimage culture, natural wonders, art islands, dancing traditions, and regional food as major themes.

The island rewards travelers who are willing to slow down and accept that transport can take more planning than in Tokyo, Kansai, or the shinkansen corridor.

How to choose a Shikoku route

  • Culture route: temples, pilgrimage towns, castles, and local festivals.
  • Nature route: valleys, rivers, coastlines, and mountain scenery.
  • Island route: art islands and Seto Inland Sea connections.
