Quick answer: what Visit Japan Web does before you fly

Visit Japan Web is the official Japanese government web service for registering arrival procedures before entering or returning to Japan. For most foreign tourists, the practical job is simple: create an account, register your passport and trip schedule, complete the immigration clearance and customs declaration fields, and show the QR code from the procedure page when you arrive. The service is useful because it lets you handle the paperwork before you are tired, carrying luggage, and trying to read airport signs after a long flight.

This page answers one specific traveler search problem: how to use the Visit Japan Web QR code for immigration and customs without confusing it with airline check-in, visas, quarantine, tax-free shopping, or unofficial apps. Official Digital Agency and Japan Customs pages were checked on 2026-07-14.

The safest default is to complete Visit Japan Web before departure, keep the official login page bookmarked, and still carry enough information to complete a paper customs declaration if your phone, battery, connection, or QR display fails. Japan Customs says paper customs forms can still be used, while electronic declaration through Visit Japan Web is recommended.

Who should use this guide

Use this guide if you are planning a first Japan trip, arriving at a major international airport, or helping a family group avoid arrival confusion. It is especially useful when the same traveler is juggling a passport, hotel address, flight number, customs answers, phone battery, mobile data, and a first airport transfer. The goal is not to make the airport feel complicated. The goal is to finish the boring part before the trip starts.

It also helps repeat visitors because Visit Japan Web has changed over time. Japan Customs notes that older 2D codes created by the Customs Declaration App are no longer effective after the January 25, 2024 unification of immigration and customs codes on Visit Japan Web.

Do not use this page as legal, visa, quarantine, animal quarantine, plant quarantine, or medication-import advice. Visit Japan Web links out to the relevant official agencies for those topics. If your trip involves medicine, food, animal products, plants, business samples, expensive goods, or unusual luggage, read the responsible official page directly before you fly.

Prepare these details before opening the form

The best time to complete Visit Japan Web is not at the baggage carousel. Do it after your flights and first hotel are stable, but before the final packing rush. The Digital Agency page says the service can be used for immigration procedures, customs declaration, and tax-free shopping service online. The setup page also notes that registration can be done on a PC, but you will need to present the 2D code at the time of procedures using a smartphone or tablet.

For most tourists, the basic preparation stack is an email address for the account, passport details, arrival flight or entry schedule, and contact information for the place of stay in Japan. If the passport scan fails, the official guide says passport information can be entered manually. Do not rush this: a typo in a name, passport number, flight date, or hotel address is much harder to fix when the line is moving.

Keep sensitive information out of shared notes. The practical goal is to know where each answer comes from: passport, airline booking, hotel confirmation, visa information if relevant, and customs declaration facts.

Step-by-step setup order

Step 1: use the official Visit Japan Web service and create or open your account. The Digital Agency says the service does not provide apps, so start from the official website rather than an app-store imitation or paid shortcut.

Step 2: register the user and passport information. If passport scanning does not work, enter the information manually. Slow down on names, birth date, passport number, nationality, and expiration date.

Step 3: register the entry or return schedule with the arrival flight, entry date, and Japan contact information from the first stay. Protect account passwords and passport details while keeping the hotel address available offline.

Step 4: fill in the entry or return procedure. The official guide places immigration clearance and customs declaration inside the procedure page. Japanese nationals and those with re-entry permits have different official notes, so follow the form shown for the traveler.

Step 5: display the QR code from the procedure page. The Digital Agency guide says it can be used at the immigration counter and at the electronic declaration terminal or inspection table for customs declaration.

How to use the QR code at the airport

At arrival, separate immigration and customs. The Digital Agency guide says the QR code can be used at the immigration counter and at the electronic declaration terminal or inspection table. Japan Customs explains that a 2D code including declaration information is created after the declaration is entered through Visit Japan Web.

If you are using an electronic customs declaration gate, the airport matters. Japan Customs says electronic customs declaration gates are available at seven international airports: New Chitose, Narita, Haneda, Chubu Centrair, Kansai, Fukuoka, and Naha. It also says that at Haneda, Fukuoka, and Naha, passengers can use electronic declaration terminals while waiting for checked baggage. Do not turn that into a universal rule for every airport or every line. Follow the signs and staff instructions on the day.

Even after electronic declaration, Japan Customs says baggage may still be inspected by customs officers. That is normal. The QR code does not make declarations optional and does not guarantee that you will walk straight through. It simply gives you a cleaner digital path when the service, airport equipment, and your phone all cooperate.

Keep a paper backup mindset. Japan Customs says the existing paper version of the customs declaration form can still be used and can also be printed on A4 in advance. You do not need to panic if technology fails, but you do need the same facts: passport, flight, address in Japan, accompanied articles, unaccompanied articles, currency or goods answers where relevant, and honest declarations.

Common mistakes to avoid

The first mistake is installing an unofficial app. The Digital Agency says Visit Japan Web does not provide apps, so start from the official service page and bookmark it yourself.

The second mistake is treating Visit Japan Web as the same thing as a visa, airline boarding pass, hotel booking, tax-free shopping plan, or customs permission for restricted goods. It is a procedure service. It does not make your passport valid, create a visa, approve restricted imports, or answer every quarantine question. Use the agency links on the official page when your case goes beyond normal tourist arrival paperwork.

The third mistake is waiting until the aircraft lands. Airport Wi-Fi, roaming setup, tired children, delayed baggage, low battery, or a forgotten password can turn a simple form into a problem. Finish the main registration before departure and keep your phone charged.

The fourth mistake is assuming the QR code replaces the customs declaration itself. Japan Customs requires all passengers entering Japan to submit the declaration. Electronic declaration is a recommended method, not a way to skip the substance of the declaration.

The fifth mistake is using old advice about the Customs Declaration App. Japan Customs says that because 2D codes for immigration and customs declarations were unified on Visit Japan Web on January 25, 2024, 2D codes created by the Customs Declaration App can no longer be effective from that date. Use the current Visit Japan Web flow.

FAQ

Is Visit Japan Web required for tourists entering Japan?

Use Visit Japan Web if you can because it is the official digital service for immigration and customs procedures, but Japan Customs still says the paper customs declaration form can be used. The right practical plan is to complete Visit Japan Web before departure and keep enough information to use the paper route if technology fails.

Is there an official Visit Japan Web app?

The Digital Agency says Visit Japan Web does not provide any apps and has warned about suspicious apps using similar names. Use the official Visit Japan Web website and avoid app-store results or paid third-party shortcuts that are not official.

What information do I need before starting Visit Japan Web?

Prepare an email address for the account, your passport, arrival or return schedule, Japan stay contact information, and customs declaration answers based on your real luggage. If passport scanning fails, the official guide says you can enter passport information manually.

Which QR code do I show at the airport?

Display the QR code from the entry or return procedure page. The Digital Agency guide says it can be used at the immigration counter for immigration clearance and at the electronic declaration terminal or inspection table for customs declaration.

Can I use a screenshot of the Visit Japan Web QR code?

For arrival procedures, the safest instruction is to open the current QR code from the procedure page on the phone you will carry. Do not depend on an old screenshot when the official page can be opened. The Digital Agency separately says screenshots cannot be used for the tax-free 2D code at stores.

What happens if my phone dies or the QR code will not load?

Do not guess or force the digital route. Japan Customs says paper customs declaration forms can still be used, so keep your arrival facts available offline and ask airport staff for the appropriate paper or inspection-table process if the phone route fails.

Does the Visit Japan Web QR code mean customs will not inspect my bag?

No. Japan Customs says baggage may still be inspected by customs officers even when passengers declare electronically. The QR code helps with the declaration workflow; it does not remove customs authority or the need to declare honestly.

Which airports have electronic customs declaration gates?

Japan Customs lists electronic customs declaration gates at seven international airports: New Chitose, Narita, Haneda, Chubu Centrair, Kansai, Fukuoka, and Naha. Follow the current airport signs and staff instructions because equipment and line handling can vary on the day.
