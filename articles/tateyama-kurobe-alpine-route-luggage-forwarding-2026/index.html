Plan the bag before the ticket

The Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route official site lists a 2026 baggage forwarding service period from April 15 to November 8, with specific eligible station and hotel pairings. It also notes that fares were current as of February 27, 2026 and may change.

That means the luggage plan is not a side detail. The route uses multiple transport modes, stairways, waiting areas, and mountain weather, so carrying large bags can make a beautiful day feel like logistics work.

Best route shapes

The cleanest use case is a one-way Toyama to Omachi or Nagano direction, or the reverse, where your overnight base matches an eligible forwarding endpoint. If you are returning to the same city, lockers may be simpler than forwarding.

Check the service counter, payment method, acceptance time, and pickup location before you buy a tight web ticket. A late bag drop can affect the whole route.

  • Use only official eligible forwarding sections when describing the service.
  • Carry weather layers, medication, and valuables with you even when forwarding the main bag.
  • Avoid a same-day long-distance hotel change if bag pickup timing is uncertain.

Who should use it

Families, photographers, and travelers crossing between Toyama and Nagano benefit most from forwarding. Backpackers with one small bag may not need it, but they should still consider weather and crowd movement at Murodo and Kurobe Dam.

If your itinerary includes Kanazawa, Toyama, Matsumoto, and Tokyo, the baggage decision should be made with hotel sequence, not only sightseeing order.

Final checks before travel

Confirm the service period, route pairings, payment method, and fare on the Alpine Route official site before departure. Do not quote forwarding options for routes that the official page does not list.

FAQ

Is Tateyama Kurobe luggage forwarding available in 2026?

The official Alpine Route page lists service from April 15 to November 8, 2026 on specified sections.

Should I forward bags or use lockers?

Forwarding is best for one-way crossings. Lockers may be simpler if you return to the same station.

Can I send luggage on any route?

No. Use only the official eligible station and hotel pairings listed for the season.
