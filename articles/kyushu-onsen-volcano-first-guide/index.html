Kyushu Onsen and Volcano First Guide

How to plan Kyushu around hot springs, volcanic landscapes, food cities, beaches, and relaxed southern travel.

Published June 18, 2026 Updated June 18, 2026 1 min read Visit Kyushu Official Travel Guide

Plan around heat, steam, and food

Official tourism information presents Kyushu as Japan's third-largest island, known for hot springs, active volcanoes, dramatic mountains, beaches, food, and a relaxed southern atmosphere.

A first route should avoid trying to cover the whole island. Choose one anchor such as Fukuoka, Beppu, Kumamoto, Nagasaki, Kagoshima, or a hot-spring town, then build outward.

First-route ideas

  • Food and city route: Fukuoka plus a nearby day trip.
  • Onsen route: Beppu, Yufuin, or another hot-spring base.
  • Volcano route: Kumamoto, Aso, or Kagoshima with weather-aware planning.
