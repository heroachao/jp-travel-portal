If you only do one thing

Treat the park as a full planning day and keep the day before or after lighter than usual.

Download the app before you enter the park

Universal Studios Japan says SUPER NINTENDO WORLD requires an Area Timed Entry Ticket: Advance Booking, an Area Timed Entry Ticket, or a Standby Entry Ticket. USJ also states that from January 5, 2026, guests need the official app to get Area Timed Entry Tickets and Standby Entry Tickets.

For Donkey Kong Country, this means the day begins before rope drop. You need park tickets ready, the app installed, and a clear decision about whether to rely on same-day app entry or buy a product that includes advance area access.

Choose the risk level

Lowest uncertainty usually means buying an Express Pass or eligible product that includes advance area entry. Lower cost usually means entering early and using the official app for free timed entry after admission. The second route can work, but it carries availability risk on busy days.

Families should also check height restrictions, restaurant timing, and whether younger children will enjoy the area if the main ride wait is long.

  • Register valid Studio Passes in the official app before trying for timed entry.
  • Do not leave Super Nintendo World as the only success condition for the whole day.
  • Keep indoor shows, food, and non-Nintendo attractions as backups for heat or crowds.

When Express Pass makes sense

Express Pass can be worth it when your Japan trip has only one Osaka theme-park day, when children care most about Nintendo, or when you are visiting on a school-holiday or weekend date. It may be less necessary for flexible adults who can arrive early and accept a backup plan.

The best answer depends on the date-specific price, the attraction lineup, and how much disappointment the group can tolerate.

Final checks before travel

Use USJ official pages for timed entry, app requirements, Express Pass details, ride operations, and temporary closures. Do not use resale or social media screenshots as the source of entry rules.

FAQ

Do I need a timed entry ticket for SUPER NINTENDO WORLD?

USJ says an advance area timed entry ticket, same-day timed entry ticket, or standby entry ticket is required, except when conditions allow otherwise.

Is the USJ app required in 2026?

USJ states that from January 5, 2026, guests need the official app to get Area Timed Entry Tickets and Standby Entry Tickets.

Should I buy Express Pass?

Consider it if Nintendo is the main reason for the visit, your date is crowded, or you cannot tolerate same-day entry risk.
