Kansai First Route Planner

A balanced way to split Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, Kobe, food, temples, and short train trips on a first Kansai visit.

Published June 18, 2026 Updated June 18, 2026 1 min read Kyoto City Official Guide
Kyoto Kansai Guides Itineraries Transport Kansai First Time Kyoto Osaka Route #kansai #kyoto #osaka

Do not split Kansai evenly by default

Kansai works because several major destinations sit close enough for short rail movements. But first-time visitors should not automatically give every city the same number of days.

Kyoto usually needs the slowest pace, Osaka works well for food and evenings, Nara is a natural day trip, and Kobe can be added when food, harbor scenery, or a more relaxed urban stop fits the trip.

Simple first split

  • Five days: three Kyoto-focused days, one Osaka day, one Nara or flexible day.
  • Seven days: add Kobe, Uji, Himeji, or a quieter temple district.
  • Use Osaka evenings for food if Kyoto mornings are reserved for temples.
