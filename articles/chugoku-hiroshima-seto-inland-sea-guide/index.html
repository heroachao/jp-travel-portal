Chugoku Hiroshima and Seto Inland Sea Guide

A first route through Hiroshima, Miyajima, Seto Inland Sea towns, beaches, forests, and peace heritage.

Build around Hiroshima and the sea

Official Japan travel guidance highlights Hiroshima city, Miyajima, the Seto Inland Sea, forest trekking, hot springs, beaches, and peace heritage. That variety means Chugoku works best as a route, not just a single museum stop.

For first-time visitors, pair Hiroshima city with Miyajima, then add one slower coastal or island element if time allows.

First-route pattern

  • Two days: Hiroshima city plus Miyajima.
  • Three or four days: add Onomichi, Tomonoura, or a Seto Inland Sea stop.
  • Longer trips: connect gardens, castle towns, beaches, and hot springs across Chugoku.

FAQ

Can Hiroshima and Miyajima fit into one day?

It is possible but rushed. Two days gives a better balance between peace heritage, food, ferries, and shrine areas.
