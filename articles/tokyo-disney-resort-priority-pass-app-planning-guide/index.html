Quick answer

Tokyo Disney Resort's official site says the free 40th Anniversary Priority Pass ends after August 31, 2026. Do not build a September or later park plan around it. Use the official app to check the services available on the exact day: Disney Premier Access is a paid, time-selected service for eligible experiences; Standby Pass can control access to selected venues; Entry Request can determine access to selected shows or experiences; mobile ordering and restaurant bookings solve different problems.

Availability, eligible experiences, prices, purchase intervals, return times, and operating conditions change. Download the official app, create the group, link or scan every park ticket, sign in, enable needed device services, and keep a payment and battery backup before park entry.

If you only do one thing: open the official “Use the App” page again on the morning of the visit and ignore old Priority Pass screenshots.

Who this guide is for

This guide is for Tokyo Disneyland or Tokyo DisneySea visitors traveling after the Priority Pass transition, families managing several tickets, groups with printed and digital tickets, and first-time visitors confused by similarly named app services.

It is not a live list of attractions, prices, or availability. The official app and park site control the day. Park admission does not guarantee any separate service, attraction, show, restaurant, or purchase.

Understand the August 31, 2026 transition

The official Priority Pass page states that the 40th Anniversary Priority Pass ends on August 31, 2026. The official app-services page repeats the transition and points visitors to other current services. Old articles and videos that describe Priority Pass as continuing indefinitely become misleading after that date.

Do not simply replace “Priority Pass” with “Premier Access.” One was a free service with its own eligibility and time assignment; the other is a paid service with a selected time for eligible experiences. The current official page lists which attractions or entertainment participate and the price for the visit date.

For travel on or around the transition, check the app in the park. The system can change by date, park, operating condition, and experience.

Prepare the official app before park day

Install the official Tokyo Disney Resort app from the verified app store listing, create or confirm the required account, update the app and phone operating system, sign in, and load the tickets or booking details. Test the password before the travel morning.

Create a group and share the relevant tickets and plans according to the official instructions. Printed tickets can be scanned where supported. Keep the original ticket code accessible; do not rely on one person's phone as the only record for the entire group.

Charge the phone, carry a permitted power bank and cable, enable needed location or notification settings, and keep mobile data. Take a screenshot only as a reference; a static image may not replace a live changing code or current app status.

Choose the first app action as a group

Before entry, rank three priorities: one must-do attraction, one show or entertainment goal, and one meal constraint. After entry, check the live app together and choose services without creating time conflicts.

Disney Premier Access may sell out and requires a separate fee beyond park admission. Standby Pass and Entry Request have their own eligibility and timing. Do not send group members to make simultaneous independent selections unless you understand how separate times or locations affect the party.

Read attraction restrictions before purchasing or obtaining access. A pass does not override height, health, supervision, or operating requirements.

Use Disney Premier Access as a budget decision

The official Premier Access page says the service is paid, applies to eligible experiences, permits a selected time, and can sell out. Check the current price and eligible experience rather than copying a list from this page. Each guest generally needs the appropriate access linked to a park ticket, subject to current child rules.

Price the whole group and compare it with the value of the time protected. A family may choose one headline experience rather than several purchases. Do not buy a time that overlaps a show, restaurant booking, hotel benefit, or another service.

Read change, cancellation, operating interruption, and refund terms before payment. Weather or technical closure does not automatically produce the outcome a traveler expects.

Use Standby Pass and Entry Request correctly

The official Standby Pass page explains that it can be required for selected venues during specified periods and allows the holder to enter the queue at a return time. It does not guarantee immediate access or operation. Current eligible venues are visible in the app.

Entry Request applies to selected shows or experiences and only successful results provide access. Do not interpret an unsuccessful request as an app error or create a schedule that has no enjoyable alternative.

Both services can be unavailable or invalidated by operational conditions. Read the displayed return time and code instructions, and arrive at the location specified by the app.

Plan meals, battery, and connectivity as park infrastructure

Mobile ordering can reduce one queue, but it introduces a pickup window and app dependence. Check dietary information with the official restaurant source and staff; a mobile photo is not an allergy guarantee. Keep water and a flexible snack within park rules.

Set the phone to a battery-conscious mode that still allows necessary app functions. Carry a permitted charger and split access responsibility across adults where the official group tools support it. Store the park ticket separately from the phone.

If the app fails, restart connectivity and seek official help. The Premier Access page identifies in-park support for guests without the app or accepted payment, but locations and hours can change. Ask a Cast Member rather than using an unofficial workaround.

Plan for families and accessibility

Keep all children assigned to adults, verify attraction restrictions before selecting a service, and avoid return times that conflict with naps, meals, or a long walk across the park. A paid or timed service does not make an unsuitable attraction suitable.

Visitors requiring accessibility support should read the official accessibility and attraction pages and contact the resort about the exact need. App services do not replace boarding or evacuation requirements.

Choose a small number of shared priorities. A family plan that preserves rest and meals often produces more value than collecting every available time slot.

FAQ

When does the Tokyo Disney Resort 40th Anniversary Priority Pass end?

The official site says it will no longer be available after August 31, 2026. Check current services in the official app.

Is Disney Premier Access the replacement for Priority Pass?

It is a different paid service for eligible experiences and selected times. Check current availability, price, and terms for the visit date.

What should I set up before park day?

Install and update the official app, sign in, create the group, link tickets, prepare payment, charge phones, and keep ticket and connectivity backups.

Does Standby Pass guarantee a short wait?

No. It can allow access to a queue for selected venues at a return time and remains subject to operation and current conditions.

Can printed and digital tickets be grouped in the app?

The official guidance explains how tickets can be scanned and shared in a group. Follow the current app instructions for every member.

Should I buy several Premier Access selections?

Only after checking the total group cost and time conflicts. One protected headline experience may fit the day better than several purchases.
