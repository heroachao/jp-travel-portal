What To Include

For a correction, include the Japan Trip Tools page URL, the statement that needs review, and a current official or primary source that supports the change.

For a technical report, include the affected URL, device and browser, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Screenshots are useful when they do not contain personal information.

Corrections And Updates

Travel information can change quickly. If you find an outdated transport note, seasonal date, facility policy, price range, or safety detail, we will review the source and update the page when appropriate.

We do not provide emergency assistance, visa decisions, travel agency booking support, or official customer service for third-party businesses.

Protect Your Personal Information

The feedback channel is public. Do not post passport details, booking references, payment information, home addresses, private email messages, or other personal data.

Japan Trip Tools does not accept paid requests to alter factual conclusions or disguise sponsored material as independent travel advice.
